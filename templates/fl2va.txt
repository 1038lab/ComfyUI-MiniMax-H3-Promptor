## Task Type: First/Last Frame (FL2VA)

This is a FIRST/LAST FRAME generation task. TWO reference images define the start and end states.

### Critical Frame Alignment

You MUST include this exact alignment directive at the very beginning of the prompt, before any section headers:

"How the reference pictures align with the target video — Picture 1 (from Shot 1) aligns with the 0.00-second mark of the target video; Picture 2 (from Shot N) aligns with the {DURATION}.00-second mark of the target video."

Replace {DURATION} with the actual video duration (e.g., 5.00, 10.00, 15.00).

### Structure Requirements

Required sections:
- Frame alignment directive (before sections)
- [BOUNDARY FRAMES] — Declare Image1 as first frame, Image2 as last frame
- [REFERENCE USE] — What each image controls
- [IDENTITY / CONTINUITY LOCKS] — Maintain consistency across the transition
- [ACTION] — Describe the single causal motion connecting the two frames
- [SHOT LIST] — Timed coverage of the transition
- [CAMERA AND SOUND] — Camera and audio for the transition
- [NEGATIVES] — No hard cuts, no teleportation

### Key Rules

1. The video MUST begin exactly matching Image1 and end exactly matching Image2
2. The transition must be ONE continuous causal motion — no teleportation
3. Strongly prefer a single long take (no cuts) to ensure smooth interpolation
4. Subject count, identity, and props must remain consistent between both frames
5. Calculate the exact duration timestamp for Image2 alignment (duration in seconds, two decimal places)
6. Avoid hard cuts, fade transitions, or cross-dissolves — these confuse the interpolation

### Example FL2VA Prompt

How the reference pictures align with the target video — Picture 1 (from Shot 1) aligns with the 0.00-second mark of the target video; Picture 2 (from Shot 4) aligns with the 15.00-second mark of the target video.

[BOUNDARY FRAMES]
Image1 is the exact first frame. Preserve its subject count, identity, wardrobe, composition, lighting, and object positions at the start.
Image2 is the exact last frame. The shot must arrive naturally at its composition, lighting, and object positions by the end.

[REFERENCE USE]
Image1 defines the starting composition, subject identity, wardrobe, and environment.
Image2 defines the ending composition, subject state, and final object positions.

[ACTION]
In one continuous causal motion, the subject transitions from the pose and state in Image1 to the pose and state in Image2. Maintain the same subject and continuous objects throughout.

[SHOT LIST]
[Shot 1] The scene opens matching Image1 exactly. The camera holds steady, establishing the starting tableau.
[Shot 2] At 00:04.000, the subject begins the primary action that will carry them toward the state in Image2. The camera tracks the movement smoothly.
[Shot 3] At 00:08.000, the transition continues with the subject now halfway between the two states. Environmental details shift accordingly.
[Shot 4] At 00:12.000, the subject arrives at the final state matching Image2. The camera settles into Image2's composition.

[CAMERA AND SOUND]
Smooth, continuous camera movement bridging Image1's framing to Image2's framing. No hard cuts. Native stereo ambience appropriate to the environment. Motivated sound effects for all physical actions.

[NEGATIVES]
No hard cut. No teleportation. No duplicate subject. No identity change. No prop swap. No discontinuous lighting. No fade or cross-dissolve transitions.
