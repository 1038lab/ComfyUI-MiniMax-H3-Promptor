## Task Type: Image-to-Audio-Video (I2VA)

This is an IMAGE-TO-VIDEO generation task. ONE reference image is provided as the first frame anchor.

### Critical First-Frame Alignment

You MUST include this exact alignment directive at the very beginning of the prompt, before any section headers:

"For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, Image1 (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced."

This tells MiniMax H3 to pixel-lock the first frame to the uploaded image.

### Structure Requirements

Required sections:
- First-frame alignment directive (before sections)
- [REFERENCE USE] — Declare what Image1 controls
- [IDENTITY / CONTINUITY LOCKS] — Lock subject identity from Image1
- [SCENE] — Describe setting and what happens after the locked first frame
- [SHOT LIST] — Start from the locked first frame, describe subsequent motion
- [ACTING] — Performance direction
- [LIGHT AND IMAGE] — Must match Image1's lighting initially, then evolve
- [CAMERA] — Camera movement starting from Image1's composition
- [PRODUCTION SOUND] — Soundscape, effects, music
- [NEGATIVES] — No identity drift from Image1

### Key Rules

1. Shot 1 MUST describe a state that precisely matches Image1
2. All subsequent action must flow naturally from Image1's pose, composition, and lighting
3. Do NOT introduce new characters not visible in Image1
4. Maintain Image1's wardrobe, environment, and props throughout

### Example I2VA Prompt

For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, Image1 (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.

[REFERENCE USE]
Image1 defines the subject's face, hair, body, wardrobe, pose, and the background environment. Preserve all visual elements exactly.

[IDENTITY / CONTINUITY LOCKS]
Keep exactly one subject. Preserve identity, wardrobe, hairstyle, and all visible props from Image1 throughout the video.

[SCENE]
A young woman in a cream linen blouse stands on a sunlit balcony overlooking a Mediterranean harbor at golden hour. She holds a ceramic coffee cup near her chest and gazes outward. A gentle sea breeze lifts strands of her dark hair.

[SHOT LIST]
0–3s — Medium shot matching Image1's exact composition; the woman is still, absorbing the view; a gentle breeze begins to lift her hair; the camera holds steady.
3–5s — The camera pushes in slowly with small amplitude; she raises the cup to her lips and takes a slow sip; her eyes close briefly in contentment; the warm light catches the steam rising from the cup.

[ACTING]
Serene, unhurried. Minimal movement — only the cup lift and hair responding to the breeze. Natural breathing, relaxed shoulders.

[LIGHT AND IMAGE]
Match Image1's golden hour lighting exactly. Warm amber tones, soft shadows, gentle lens flare from the low sun. Shallow depth of field with the harbor softly bokeh'd behind her.

[CAMERA]
Begin locked to Image1's framing. Only a gentle, slow push-in is permitted. No sudden movements or dramatic angle changes.

[PRODUCTION SOUND]
Gentle harbor ambience: distant lapping water, far-off boat horns, light wind. Ceramic cup tink when she lifts it. No music. No dialogue.

[NEGATIVES]
No identity drift from Image1. No wardrobe changes. No extra people. No abrupt camera movements. No subtitles.
