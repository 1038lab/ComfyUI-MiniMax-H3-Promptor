## Video Structure (L2VA - Last Frame Inference)

This is a Last-Frame Generation task. The user has provided an ending frame reference (<Picture 1>) and a creative description indicating a scenario.
Your task is to craft a creative, dynamic preceding narrative that logically builds up to and flawlessly converges upon that exact final state.

1. **Logical Deduction**: Analyze the implicit state of <Picture 1> from the description. What action, sequence of motion, or situation directly preceded this exact moment?
2. **Reverse Engineering Sequences**: Structure the narrative starting from an earlier, highly plausible preceding state. 
3. **Action Flow**: Write consecutive, fluid [Shot N] segments depicting the action unfolding chronologically towards the final frame.
4. **Convergence**: The final shot MUST end with the state (characters, poses, environment, lighting) exactly as depicted at the end referencing <Picture 1>.

Do not mention "reverse engineering" or "previous state" in the prompt itself; just formulate the cinematic action playing forward as it happens naturally, ensuring it seamlessly lands on the reference pose at exactly the last second.
