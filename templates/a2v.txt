## Task Type: Audio-to-Video (A2V)

This is an AUDIO-TO-VIDEO generation task. NO IMAGE OR VIDEO is provided. ONLY AUDIO is provided as a reference.

### Structure Requirements

Required sections:
- [REFERENCE USE] — Declare that the Audio dictates the flow, rhythm, or content of the scene.
- [SCENE] — Describe the setting, characters, and narrative arc that fits the audio mood.
- [SHOT LIST] — Timed visual coverage with camera and action, reacting to audio cues.
- [ACTING] — Performance direction reacting to the sound or voice.
- [LIGHT AND IMAGE] — Visual treatment reflecting the audio's emotional tone.
- [CAMERA] — Camera movements complementing the audio rhythm.
- [PRODUCTION SOUND] — Explicitly map visual sound sources (e.g. lips moving, objects hitting) to the provided audio reference.
- [NEGATIVES] — Likely failure modes to avoid.

### Key Rules

1. This is a generative prompt that must build a world AROUND the provided sound.
2. The [PRODUCTION SOUND] and [SHOT LIST] must heavily emphasize synchronization with the Audio reference.
