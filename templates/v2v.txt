## Task Type: Video-to-Video (V2V)

This is a VIDEO-TO-VIDEO generation task. A reference video is provided.
NO AUDIO is referenced.

### Structure Requirements

Required sections:
- [REFERENCE USE] — Declare what aspects of the Video to follow (motion, composition, subject).
- [IDENTITY / CONTINUITY LOCKS] — Lock subject identity and style across frames.
- [SCENE] — Describe setting and overall motion flow.
- [SHOT LIST] — Describe the motion timing corresponding to the reference video flow.
- [ACTING] — Performance direction matching the video flow.
- [LIGHT AND IMAGE] — Visual style and lighting.
- [CAMERA] — Camera movement.
- [NEGATIVES] — Undesired elements.

### Key Rules

1. You must describe the continuous motion and continuity across the full video segment.
2. Maintain spatial and temporal coherence.
3. The prompt should augment or style the reference motion.
4. Do NOT include a [PRODUCTION SOUND] section.
