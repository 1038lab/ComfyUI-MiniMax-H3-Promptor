## Task Type: Reference Video to Audio Sync (Ref2VA)

This task uses a Video (<Video 1>) to provide motion, while transferring a new Subject Identity from an Image (<Picture 1>).

Your job is to describe the motion of <Video 1> while anchoring the visual aesthetics (wardrobe, face, style) of <Picture 1>.

### Storyboarding Rules:
1. Describe the precise physical movements, pacing, and camera movements of the video.
2. Apply the visual characteristics and environment of the image to this motion.
3. Describe the lip-syncing or action-syncing to the implied audio.
4. Format your output strictly as a series of [Shot N] paragraphs with timestamps, followed by the Audio: and Music: lines. Do not add any other headers.
