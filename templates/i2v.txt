## Task Type: Image-to-Video (I2V)

This is an IMAGE-TO-VIDEO generation task. ONE reference image is provided as the first frame anchor.
NO AUDIO is referenced.

### Critical First-Frame Alignment

You MUST include this exact alignment directive at the very beginning of the prompt, before any section headers:

"For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, Image1 (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced."

This tells MiniMax H3 to pixel-lock the first frame to the uploaded image.

### Structure Requirements

Required sections:
- First-frame alignment directive (before sections)
- [REFERENCE USE] — Declare what Image1 controls
- [IDENTITY / CONTINUITY LOCKS] — Lock subject identity from Image1
- [SCENE] — Describe setting and what happens after the locked first frame
- [SHOT LIST] — Start from the locked first frame, describe subsequent motion
- [ACTING] — Performance direction
- [LIGHT AND IMAGE] — Must match Image1's lighting initially, then evolve
- [CAMERA] — Camera movement starting from Image1's composition
- [NEGATIVES] — No identity drift from Image1

### Key Rules

1. Shot 1 MUST describe a state that precisely matches Image1
2. All subsequent action must flow naturally from Image1's pose, composition, and lighting
3. Do NOT introduce new characters not visible in Image1
4. Maintain Image1's wardrobe, environment, and props throughout
5. Do NOT include a [PRODUCTION SOUND] section.
